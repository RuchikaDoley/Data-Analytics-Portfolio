Superstore Sales Analysis | Power BI

An 11-page Power BI report analysing retail sales performance. The data is stored inside the report file, so open `superstore_sales_analysis.pbix` in Power BI Desktop to explore it.

Pages:
Sales by category, year, ship mode, customer name, state, segment, product name, city and sub-category, a headline sales gauge, and a combined dashboard page.

Visuals used:
Map, gauge, donut and pie charts, stacked area chart, clustered column charts, a combo (column and line) chart and a customer table.

What the report shows:
- Which states and cities bring in the most sales
- How sales change over the years
- How sales split across categories, sub-categories, segments and ship modes
- Which customers and products sell the most

Tools:
Power BI Desktop
